# Playlist, Series & Channel Architecture

## Purpose

Channel architecture helps viewers understand what belongs together and what they should watch next.

## Playlists

Use playlists when grouping improves navigation or viewing continuity. Names should describe the viewer's destination or subject. Order episodes intentionally when sequence matters.

## Series

A series creates a repeatable expectation. Define audience, premise, episode format, naming convention, visual identity, and progression. Middle episodes should remain understandable to new viewers.

## Channel organization

Organize around audience needs rather than internal production structure. Feature representative content, clear entry points, and useful next steps.

## Content pathways

For each important video, ask what a satisfied viewer should watch next, whether that next step is actually relevant, whether it is easy to find, and whether it continues or deepens the viewer's problem.

## Avoid over-architecture

Too many playlists, overlapping categories, or inconsistent naming can make a channel harder to understand. Organization should reduce cognitive load.

## Format strategy

Different formats can coexist when their roles are clear. Shorts may support discovery, long-form may provide depth, and live content may support interaction. Do not assume every format has the same job.

## Principle

Architecture succeeds when viewers can answer what should I watch next without needing the creator to explain the structure.
