# Revenue & Monetization

## Purpose

Creator revenue is a system of different income sources with different eligibility rules, economics, risks, and measurement windows.

## Revenue categories

Depending on eligibility and region, revenue can include advertising, YouTube Premium-related revenue, memberships, fan funding and live features, shopping or affiliate activity, sponsorships, products and services, licensing, and other rights-based income.

## Think in systems

Analyze revenue alongside audience, content, format, traffic source, seasonality, and production cost. High views do not necessarily mean high profit; a smaller audience with strong commercial intent can be valuable.

## Important distinctions

Do not confuse revenue with profit, RPM with CPM, estimated revenue with finalized payments, sponsorship value with creator take-home, or short-term spikes with durable income.

## Monetization risk

Protect the audience relationship. Aggressive sponsorship frequency, misleading claims, unauthorized reused material, or content designed solely around monetization can create long-term costs.

## Revenue workflow

Record revenue by source and period, content where attribution is meaningful, direct costs, and major external factors. Compare normalized periods rather than reacting to one anomalous day.

## Principle

The strongest monetization strategy is usually diversified and audience-aligned. Treat revenue as an operating system around the content business, not as a substitute for useful content.
