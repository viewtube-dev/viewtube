# Comments, Community & Audience Feedback

## Purpose

Audience feedback is qualitative evidence. It can reveal confusion, unmet needs, objections, language, recurring questions, and ideas, but it is not a statistically representative sample by default.

## Read comments carefully

Group comments into questions, praise, confusion, corrections, requests, objections, stories, and spam. Look for repeated patterns rather than treating the loudest comment as truth.

## Feedback loop

Collect feedback, cluster themes, estimate frequency and relevance, verify factual claims independently, convert strong themes into hypotheses, test them, and record outcomes.

## Community interaction

Respond when the response adds value. Pin useful clarifications when appropriate. Do not manufacture conflict to increase activity. Protect personal information and moderate harassment or dangerous content.

## Corrections

When a material factual error is identified, verify it and correct it clearly. Preserve an audit trail for important corrections.

## Productive questions

Audience questions can become future videos, FAQ sections, experiments, or research prompts. Capture them in a structured backlog rather than relying on memory.

## Principle

Community is both a relationship and a research channel. Listen systematically, but do not mistake anecdote for aggregate evidence.
