# Live Streaming Operations

## Purpose

Live video combines content creation, real-time production, audience interaction, moderation, and operational reliability.

## Before the stream

Define purpose, audience promise, run of show, start and end window, guests, assets, moderation plan, and fallback plan. Test camera, microphone, network, overlays, scenes, permissions, and recording settings.

## Run of show

A useful structure includes opening and orientation, immediate value, main segments, audience interaction, recurring checkpoints, and closing. Avoid spending the opening entirely on setup or waiting for an audience.

## Chat and moderation

Establish rules before going live. Assign moderators when appropriate. Prepare responses for spam, harassment, personal information, dangerous requests, and technical incidents.

## Technical resilience

Have fallback communication, backup assets, and a recovery plan. Know how to restart or communicate a delay without improvising under pressure.

## After the stream

Review the recording, audience behavior, peak and sustained participation, recurring questions, technical problems, and moments worth turning into clips or follow-up content.

## Repurposing

A stream can become clips, Shorts, tutorials, FAQs, highlights, or future episode ideas. Preserve context so excerpts do not create misleading impressions.

## Principle

Live quality comes from preparation plus adaptability. The goal is reliable delivery of a clear promise while maintaining healthy audience interaction.
