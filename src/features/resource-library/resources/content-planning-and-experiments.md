# Content Planning & Experiments

## Purpose

Content planning converts audience needs, creator goals, evidence, and available resources into a sequence of testable ideas.

## Idea sources

Useful inputs include audience questions, analytics patterns, search demand, recommendation adjacency, category observations, content gaps, creator expertise, timely events, reusable assets, and business goals.

## Idea record

Capture each idea with working title, audience, problem or promise, format, evidence, production cost, dependencies, risks, and intended success criteria.

## Prioritization

Balance potential value against confidence, effort, timing, and strategic fit. Record why an idea is believed to have potential rather than hiding uncertainty in a score.

## Experiment design

State the hypothesis, change, target audience, expected signal, measurement window, comparison, and decision rule. Change one important variable when possible.

## Learn from failure

A weak result is useful when it narrows uncertainty. Record what was tested, what happened, what was learned, and whether the hypothesis should be revised, repeated, or retired.

## Content portfolio

Not every video needs the same purpose. A healthy portfolio can include discovery, depth, relationship, experimentation, evergreen, timely, and monetization-oriented content.

## Principle

Planning is not prediction. It is disciplined uncertainty management: make better bets, learn from evidence, and improve the next decision.
