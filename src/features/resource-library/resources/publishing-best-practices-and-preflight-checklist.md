# Publishing Best Practices and Preflight Checklist

A repeatable final-check workflow for preparing, scheduling, publishing and verifying videos.

> **Creator principle:** Publishing is a quality-control handoff, not just pressing Publish.

## The Creator Mental Model

Publishing is a quality-control handoff, not just pressing Publish.

## What This Means for Your Channel

Review the final export, audio, captions, spelling, links, privacy and rights before upload. Keep a known-good export so revisions are traceable.

## Core Concepts

Write a specific title, put useful context early in the description, and use chapters when they improve navigation. Tags are secondary to clear topic, title, thumbnail and video.

## Diagnose Real Creator Questions

Verify visibility, schedule and time zone; audience and disclosure settings; thumbnail; playlist; captions; chapters; end screens/cards; monetization and rights. Re-open the processed video and confirm playback and links after release.

## Creator Action Toolbox

- [ ] Define the audience, goal and question.
- [ ] Check source, context, definitions and time window.
- [ ] Separate observed evidence from possible explanations.
- [ ] Choose one proportionate action and record why.
- [ ] Set a review window and note what would change your mind.

## Common Misinterpretations and Myths

Do not treat a single metric, comment, video or short-term fluctuation as a universal rule. Platform definitions and features can change. Separate official documentation from inference, and note uncertainty when the evidence cannot establish a cause.

## Quick Reference

| Question | First action |
|---|---|
| What changed? | Define the metric, scope and comparison |
| Why might it have changed? | List multiple plausible explanations |
| What should I do? | Choose a test that fits the evidence |
| Did it help? | Review after a suitable window |

## Glossary

**Hypothesis** — A plausible explanation that can be checked against evidence.  
**Scope** — The videos, audience, format, source or other items included in a report.  
**Time window** — The period represented by the data.  
**Inference** — An explanation drawn from observations rather than directly reported as fact.
