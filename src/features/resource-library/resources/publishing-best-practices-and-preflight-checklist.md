# Publishing Best Practices and Preflight Checklist

## Purpose

Publishing is the handoff between finished content and the platform's ability to understand, distribute, and learn from it. This guide turns publishing into a repeatable quality-control process.

## Before upload

Confirm the video delivers the promise made by its concept and packaging. Verify the final edit, audio, captions, chapters where useful, links, disclosures, rights, and export quality. Remove accidental private information, unfinished frames, duplicate uploads, and unsupported claims.

## Metadata

Write a title that is specific about the viewer outcome or subject. Write a description that gives useful context in natural language and put important information first. Add chapters when they improve navigation. Use relevant language rather than keyword stuffing. Tags are secondary and should not substitute for clear topic, title, thumbnail, and video.

## Packaging check

Review title and thumbnail together at small size. Ask whether the subject is immediately understandable, there is a clear reason to click, the package accurately represents the video, and the promise survives mobile display.

## Final preflight

Check visibility, thumbnail, title, description and links, captions, end screens or cards, playlist placement, monetization and disclosures, copyright and rights, schedule, and privacy. Watch the processed upload before release when the workflow permits.

## After publishing

Do not judge performance from the first few minutes alone. Observe exposure, click behavior, retention, traffic sources, audience fit, and satisfaction evidence over an appropriate window. Record meaningful anomalies and experiments.

## Operating principle

Automate repetitive checks; reserve human review for meaning, accuracy, audience fit, rights, and the actual promise of the content.
